# 💖 Our Universe of Love - Surprise Website for Lovers

An intimate, unique, and romantic surprise web experience crafted especially for couples and lovers.

---

## 🌟 Unique Features Included:

1. **🔒 Heartbeat Anniversary Vault**:
   - An interactive lock with an accelerating heartbeat animation and synthesized `lub-dub` heartbeat sound.
   - Default PIN: `1402` (Customizable in `js/config.js`).
   - Hint button included.

2. **⭐ The Constellation of Us (Milestone Journey)**:
   - 5 cosmic milestones (*The Day We Met*, *Our First Late Night Call*, *First Date*, *The Day I Knew You Were The One*, *Today & Eternity*).
   - Interactive expanding cards with photos and heartfelt memories.

3. **🎵 Retro Vinyl Turntable Mixtape**:
   - Realistic spinning vinyl record with needle arm drop animation.
   - Plays AR Rahman's *"Avalum Naanum"* (or any custom song) with real-time bouncing audio visualizer frequency bars.

4. **🍯 The Jar of Endless Love (3D Origami Notes)**:
   - A glowing glass jar filled with 3D paper hearts.
   - Tap or shake the jar to unfold 20+ sweet, cute, and romantic reasons why you love your partner.

5. **💘 Couples Telepathy Mini-Game**:
   - Playful interactive questions (*"Who fell in love first?", "Who says sorry first?"*) with affectionate romantic response bubbles and a soulmate crown verdict.

6. **📸 Frozen in Time (Polaroid Memory Wall)**:
   - Vintage pinned polaroids with washi tape, authentic shadows, and a click-to-zoom Lightbox modal.

7. **💌 Wax-Sealed Vintage Love Letter & Sky Lantern Finale**:
   - A 3D crimson wax seal that cracks and opens upon clicking.
   - An unfolding parchment letter with a graceful typewriter handwriting effect.
   - Smoothly transitions background music to an emotional piano melody.
   - **Lanterns Finale**: Release glowing sky lanterns into the night sky to make an eternal wish together.

## 🛠️ How to Customize:

Open [`js/config.js`](file:///C:/Users/ELCOT/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/lovers-surprise/js/config.js) in any text editor to customize:
* **Partner's Name & Nickname**: Change `"My Chella Kutty"` and `"Bujji 🫀"`.
* **Secret Date / PIN**: Change `"1402"` to your anniversary date or birthday (e.g., `2407`).
* **Your Name**: Change `"Yours Forever"`.
* **Photos**: Put any of your favorite photos in `assets/images/` and update filenames in `config.js`.
* **Music**: Put any romantic song MP3 in `assets/audio/`.
* **Reasons & Letter**: Edit the text directly in `config.js` to write your personal love story!

---

## 🚀 How to Run / Open:

Simply double-click [`index.html`](file:///C:/Users/ELCOT/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/lovers-surprise/index.html) in your browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Safari, etc.)!
